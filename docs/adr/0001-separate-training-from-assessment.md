# Separate adaptive training from standardized assessment

Product Drill will keep adaptive training and standardized assessment as separate product flows with separate task pools and evidence rules. Training may personalize challenges and provide assistance, while the core independent judgment assessment must be versioned, unassisted, comparable, and protected from training exposure; combining them would make personalization contaminate score comparability and encourage teaching to the test.

Assessment outputs remain diagnostic reports until their intended employment use is supported by job relevance, score consistency, predictive evidence, fairness, and auditable development records. Training performance must not be repurposed as a hiring score.

An AI-augmented work sample may be offered as a separate assessment module using a controlled and fully recorded AI tool. Its result must be reported separately and must not be combined with or substitute for independent judgment evidence.
