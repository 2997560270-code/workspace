# Govern community review as provisional evidence

Community blind reviews must include a rubric judgment, evidence references, rationale, and confidence. AI may aggregate reviews and explain disagreement, but it must preserve the original reviews and must not turn an unqualified majority into an official assessment result; community conclusions remain provisional until supported by verified reviewers and employment-use validity evidence.

Reasonable-or-unreasonable quality votes affect reviewer reliability and re-review routing, never the learner's result directly. Review contribution credits are capped, non-transferable, quality-gated rewards for hosted training usage and cannot be exchanged for official assessment results or score advantages.
