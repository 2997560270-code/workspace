# Open the training core and control official assessment

Product Drill will open-source its training application, community training scenarios, self-hosting path, and BYOK support so learners and contributors can use and improve the training system without depending on the hosted service. Self-hosted and BYOK deployments may produce diagnostic results, but those results are unofficial.

The official assessment task pool, calibration data, fixed model and scoring conditions, and verifiable result issuance will remain in a controlled service. Publishing or allowing user-controlled versions of those assets would expose secure tasks, break score comparability, and allow modified deployments to issue results that appear official.
