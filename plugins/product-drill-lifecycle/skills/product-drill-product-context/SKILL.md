# Product Drill Product Context

## Required context

Read [references/direction-a.md](references/direction-a.md) before planning or changing Product Drill.

## Decision rules

1. Optimize for product newcomers, career switchers, and PMs with 0–3 years of experience.
2. Protect the loop: diagnosis → scenario interview → product judgment → evidence feedback → local retry → improvement evidence.
3. Prefer observable behavior and cited conversation evidence over decorative scores.
4. AI simulation is practice, not proof of market demand.
5. One scenario has one primary skill. Secondary skills may be observed but must not dominate feedback.
6. Do not add enterprise administration, community, courses, payments, or a generic product-advice surface unless explicitly scoped.
7. The north-star unit is a user completing training, viewing evidence, retrying a weakness, and improving.

## Before implementation

State the target user, target skill, evidence produced, success event, and explicit non-goals. If any is missing, resolve it from repository context before editing.
