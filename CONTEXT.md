# Product Drill

Product Drill exists to develop and prove product judgment for aspiring product managers, career switchers, and people taking on product-decision responsibilities. It does not perform product discovery on the learner's behalf.

## Language

**Product judgment**:
The learner's ability to make and justify evidence-based decisions in unfamiliar product-discovery situations.
_Avoid_: Training score, document quality, AI answer quality

**Product discovery judgment**:
The bounded part of product judgment used to determine whether a product problem is real, consequential, and worth testing before committing to a solution.
_Avoid_: Overall PM competency, interview performance, framework recall

**Judgment habit hypothesis**:
An evidence-backed, falsifiable claim about how a learner tends to decide under defined conditions. It remains provisional until tested across unfamiliar situations.
_Avoid_: Ability score, personality label, permanent trait

**Premature solution commitment**:
A judgment habit in which the learner commits to a solution before gathering enough evidence about the current workflow, problem consequences, and existing alternatives.
_Avoid_: Fast decision-making, solution ideation

**Adaptive challenge loop**:
A sequence of learner decisions and consequences selected to test the most uncertain, consequential judgment habit hypothesis next.
_Avoid_: Fixed lesson, mandatory stage sequence, content playlist

**Causal scenario world**:
A governed product-discovery environment with stable hidden facts, stakeholder incentives, constraints, and causal consequences against which learner decisions can be tested.
_Avoid_: AI role-play prompt, static case study, unconstrained simulation

**Decision event**:
A learner's committed action, expected outcome, confidence, and rejected alternative, captured before its consequences are revealed.
_Avoid_: Chat turn, keyword match, form completion

**Independent evidence**:
A decision event committed before the learner receives guidance about that decision.
_Avoid_: Prompted answer, coached retry

**Assisted evidence**:
A decision event influenced by a hint or intervention. It can show learning progress but cannot establish independent judgment.
_Avoid_: Independent performance

**Within-world correction**:
Improved behavior after feedback in the same causal scenario world. It shows that feedback was understood, not that judgment transferred.
_Avoid_: Ability improvement, transfer

**Simulation transfer evidence**:
Independent evidence from a new causal scenario world that tests the same judgment habit hypothesis through different surface details.
_Avoid_: Retry score, repeated-case performance

**Real-work transfer evidence**:
A decision event and observed outcome from the learner's real product work that tests a judgment habit hypothesis.
_Avoid_: Self-reported confidence, simulated performance

**Judgment evidence graph**:
The traceable relationships among a learner's judgment habit hypotheses, triggering conditions, decision events, interventions, consequences, and transfer evidence.
_Avoid_: Ability radar, score history, transcript archive

**Calibration aggregate**:
A consented, de-identified summary of behavior across learners used to estimate challenge quality and intervention effectiveness without exposing source content.
_Avoid_: Shared transcript, public learner profile, raw-content training set

**World steward**:
A product-domain expert accountable for the truth boundaries and quality of causal scenario worlds, but not for delivering training or grading individual learners.
_Avoid_: Tutor, coach, individual grader

**Calibration sprint**:
A learner's first adaptive sequence of decision events, used to establish initial judgment habit hypotheses and expose what remains uncertain.
_Avoid_: Self-assessment, placement quiz, onboarding questionnaire

**Next best challenge**:
The challenge expected to reduce the most consequential uncertainty in a learner's judgment evidence graph next.
_Avoid_: Recommended content, next lesson, daily task

**Judgment evidence profile**:
The learner-facing view of traceable judgment habit hypotheses, confidence, evidence conditions, and transfer status.
_Avoid_: Ability score, leaderboard rank, personality report

**Judgment evidence packet**:
A learner-selected, reviewable set of decision chains that discloses context, consequences, transfer evidence, and AI assistance for external review.
_Avoid_: AI certificate, generated portfolio claim, universal PM credential

**Standardized assessment**:
A separate, versioned, unassisted evaluation that uses equivalent causal scenario worlds to produce comparable quantitative evidence.
_Avoid_: Training session, adaptive coaching, AI-generated score

**Assessment result**:
The quantitative evidence, uncertainty, conditions, and version produced by a standardized assessment. It is not a credential until external validity is established.
_Avoid_: Universal PM score, certification without validation

**Diagnostic report**:
An evidence-based summary of observed product discovery judgment used for learning and pilot feedback, not for hiring or promotion decisions.
_Avoid_: Hiring score, percentile, credential

**Validated assessment metric**:
An assessment result whose intended employment use is supported by job relevance, consistency, predictive evidence, fairness, and auditable development records.
_Avoid_: Pilot index, expert-opinion score, unvalidated ranking

**Product discovery judgment level**:
A criterion-referenced level describing the product discovery decisions a candidate can demonstrate under stated assessment conditions, reported with uncertainty.
_Avoid_: Percentage correct, universal pass line, overall PM level

**Cohort percentile**:
A candidate's relative position within a named comparison population for a specific assessment version.
_Avoid_: Permanent rank, population-independent ability

**Independent judgment assessment**:
A standardized assessment module completed without AI guidance to measure the candidate's own product discovery judgment.
_Avoid_: AI-assisted response, coached assessment

**AI-augmented work sample**:
A separately reported assessment module in which the candidate uses a controlled, fully recorded AI tool and remains accountable for validating its output and making the final decision.
_Avoid_: Independent judgment score, unrestricted take-home task

**Seasonal challenge ranking**:
An opt-in ranking for a named season and comparison group using a dedicated competition task pool. It supports participation and task piloting but is not an employment assessment result.
_Avoid_: Training activity leaderboard, permanent global rank, hiring rank

**Official assessment result**:
A verifiable assessment result issued through the governed official assessment service using controlled task, model, scoring, and version conditions.
_Avoid_: Self-hosted score, BYOK score, community certificate

**Unofficial assessment result**:
A result produced by a self-hosted, BYOK, or community deployment for learning and experimentation, without official employment-use claims.
_Avoid_: Official credential, verified hiring score

**Verified assessment session**:
An official assessment session sponsored by an approved institution or employer with verified identity, controlled conditions, process records, and human review of anomalies.
_Avoid_: Open self-test, automated biometric proctoring, unverified remote result

**Community blind review**:
An anonymized evaluation of a decision event that includes a rubric judgment, evidence references, rationale, and confidence while hiding the learner's identity and other reviews.
_Avoid_: Popular vote, anonymous score without rationale

**Verified reviewer**:
A reviewer whose reliability is supported by performance on hidden anchor cases and stable evidence-based review behavior over time.
_Avoid_: Self-declared expert, popularity rank, paid authority

**Provisional community diagnosis**:
An AI-aggregated view of community blind reviews that preserves disagreement and remains unsuitable for employment decisions until stronger reviewer and validity evidence exists.
_Avoid_: Official assessment result, majority verdict

**Review quality vote**:
An anonymized reasonable-or-unreasonable judgment about a blind review's use of evidence and rubric. It affects reviewer reliability and re-review routing, never the learner's result directly.
_Avoid_: Candidate vote, like count, score adjustment

**Review contribution credit**:
A capped, non-transferable allowance for hosted training usage granted only after a blind review passes evidence, anchor, and quality checks.
_Avoid_: Cash reward, tradeable token, assessment score credit
